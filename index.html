Q&A

Do I need to download an app?

No. Oli works in your browser on any phone or computer.

How does Oli use my taste profile?

Every search is cross-checked against your saved tastes, dietary profile and family needs, so results and dishes fit you.

Where do the ratings come from?

Oli combines scores from several review platforms into one number out of 5. In this prototype the figures are simulated.

Can I search for any restaurant name?

Yes. Type any name and Oli builds a tailored result for it.
